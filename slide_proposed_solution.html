• Proposed Solution: Past Systems Tried vs What EcoSync Fixes

Analyzing historical policy failures across Himalayan and Western Ghat corridors to build an offline-first, tamper-proof DPI.

❌ Past Systems Tried & Why They Failed
1. Static PDF & Web Portals (Rohtang / Ooty) Severely Abused

Online portals issue static PDF permits with static QR codes.

Why it failed: Tourists took screenshots, forwarded WhatsApp PDFs, or bought cloned passes from local touts at 10x markups.
2. Manual Paper Toll Registers Human Bottleneck

Guards physically hand-write vehicle numbers and passenger counts at barriers.

Why it failed: 2-3 minutes per vehicle caused 14-hour traffic jams in Lonavala and Kullu. Zero real-time data sync with district collectors.
3. Cloud-Only App Portals (Firebase/Supabase) Network Failure

Required guards' handheld scanners to hit cloud servers for verification.

Why it failed: Himalayan deadzones (zero 4G/5G) broke scanner apps. Toll barriers had to be opened manually, collapsing quotas.
4. Macro-Only Town Level Limits Zero Micro-Control

Cap placed on the entire hill station (e.g. Mahabaleshwar / Shimla).

Why it failed: Tourists congregated simultaneously at singular micro-spots (e.g., Tiger Point, Mall Road), triggering landslides & stampedes.
✅ The EcoSync Architecture: What We Are Fixing
🔒

Dynamic Rotating Ed25519 QR

Replaces static PDFs with self-rotating QR tokens using 30-second epoch-salted hashes signed by the Authority Private Key.

The Fix: Screenshots & forwarded passes expire within 30s. Stops tout black-marketing permanently.
📶

100% Offline Edge Verification

Checkposts verify passes mathematically using pre-distributed Ed25519 public keys—without needing active cellular connectivity.

The Fix: Sub-300ms verification works seamlessly in deep Himalayan network blindspots.
🚗

NHAI FASTag RFID Integration

Integrates directly into existing National Electronic Toll Collection (NETC) RFID transponders for automatic barrier lifts.

The Fix: Zero stop-and-go manual paper logging. Eliminates 14-hr highway gridlocks with ₹0 new toll hardware.
🎯

Micro-Spot Granularity & Yield

Geofences individual sensitive tourist spots (viewpoints, waterfalls) with 20% carrying-capacity discount vouchers to divert crowds.

The Fix: Balances tourist density across secondary attractions before ecological damage occurs.
Outcome: From Fragmented & Easily Cloned Passes ➔ To Decentralized, Offline-First DPI Governance. DPI Innovation
